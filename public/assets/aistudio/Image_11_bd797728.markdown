## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a campus community navigating stressful moments: losing an essential item or striving to return one. It combines classical academic prestige with the warmth, clarity, and approachability of modern community services.

### Emotional Disposition
- **Reassurance & Trust:** Solid, institutional hues remove frantic panic from item loss.
- **Clarity & Efficiency:** Zero extraneous ornamentation; users must scan, log, and claim items rapidly.
- **Warm Civic Hospitality:** Natural, tactile tones replace clinical corporate palettes, encouraging collective campus responsibility.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Warm Institutional Minimalism:** Employs expansive off-white and warm cream canvas foundations layered with crisp white cards, grounded in deep forest greens and earthen terracotta accents.
- **Tactile Softness:** Defined by physical warmth—avoiding aggressive glassmorphism, cold tech blues, or fluorescent neon accents. Hierarchy relies on structural border contrasts, gentle ambient shadows, and purposeful warm color tagging.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from historic campus courtyards, library brickwork, and park lawns, maintaining strict WCAG AAA contrast ratios for body typography.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#1B4332`):** Deep collegiate forest green. Communicates authority, trust, and official campus resolution. Used for primary CTA buttons, dominant titles, active navigation states, and primary focal highlights.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32`):** Terracotta / warm rust clay. Used for urgent actions (e.g., "Report Lost Item"), high-priority alerts, and focused interaction badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Warm amber gold. Expresses "In Review," "Pending Match," or "Awaiting Pickup" states.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1B2A22`):** Deep pine graphite. Replaces pure black to preserve warmth while guaranteeing crisp readability for body copy, table text, and icons.

### Surface Tones & Tonal Accents
- **Canvas / Root Background:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream) with `#F4F1EA` for recessed sections and sidebars.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary Surface) and `#F8F6F0` (Secondary/Linen Container).
- **Hairline Borders:** `#E7E3D8` and `#E5E0D5`.
- **Status Soft Tints:**
  - *Success / Resolved:* Surface `#D8F3DC`, Foreground `#2D6A4F`
  - *Alert / Lost Item:* Surface `#FDE8E1`, Foreground `#C85A32`
  - *Pending / Review:* Surface `#FEF3C7`, Foreground `#92400E`

## Typography

The type architecture relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric curves, high x-height, and humane open apertures.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for dashboard welcome headers, public search portals, and hero callouts. Dense letter-spacing creates an authoritative, editorial balance.
- **Headlines & Titles:** Distinguish item categories, claim ticket statuses, and modal dialogues. 
- **Body:** Kept at a generous line height for effortless scanning across high-volume listings and descriptions of found objects.
- **Labels:** Rendered with elevated weight (`600`) and slight tracking to anchor metadata badges, timestamps, pickup location markers, and table schema headers.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid system organizes discovery grids, item claim queues, and data entry forms.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width of `1280px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Sidebar controls for filter facets lock at `280px` or `320px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Filter rails collapse into sliding drawer sheets.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` margins. Item feeds switch into single-column vertical card flows.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component spacing follows a modular 4px/8px scale:
- `space-xs` (`4px`): Micro badge padding and inline icon gap.
- `space-sm` (`8px`): Form label to input offset, tag spacing.
- `space-md` (`16px`): Standard card padding on mobile, form field vertical stacks.
- `space-lg` (`24px`): Default card padding on desktop, section header margins.
- `space-xl` (`40px`): Section vertical breaks and boundary gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic digital finishes, depth is articulated through **tonal separation** paired with **warm ambient drop shadows**.

### Elevation Scale
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Canvas `#FAF8F5`. No shadow. Recessed inputs and inactive table backgrounds use `#F4F1EA`.
- **Surface Level (Level 1):** Primary Cards and Item Panels (`#FFFFFF`). Outlined by `border border-[#E5E0D5]` and softened with `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Hover (Level 2):** Hovered Item Cards, Floating Quick Actions, and Tooltips. Lifted with `0 4px 6px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.04), 0 10px 20px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.06)`, maintaining the warm border.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals (Level 3):** Verification Modals, Claim Proof Sheets, and Dropdowns. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 30% tinted pine graphite scrim (`#1B2A224D`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature to soften institutional administrative tools.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Input fields, inline filter chips, table rows, and secondary buttons.
- **Card Corner Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Item detail cards, summary statistic panels, and upload drop zones.
- **Large Surface Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Main dialogue modals, flyout drawers, and search banner wrappers.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Status indicator tags, round avatar placeholders, and floating status pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1B4332`, Text `#FFFFFF`, Corner radius `rounded-lg` (`8px`), Padding `10px 20px`. Hover state transitions to `#2D6A4F`.
- **Secondary / Action (Report Lost):** Background `#C85A32`, Text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state transitions to `#D97443`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, Border `1px solid #E5E0D5`, Text `#1B2A22`. Hover background `#F4F1EA`.

### Cards & Item Feed Tiles
- Structural boundary with `1px solid #E7E3D8` over `#FFFFFF`.
- Features an image wrapper ratio of `4:3` with subtle inner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Metadata stack displays an inline building badge (e.g., "Student Union 2nd Floor") along with human-readable timestamps ("Found 2h ago").

### Chips & Badges
- **Claim Status Tags:** Pill-shaped with `4px 10px` padding, typography `label-sm`.
  - *Found / Available:* Background `#D8F3DC`, Text `#1B4332`.
  - *Reported Lost:* Background `#FDE8E1`, Text `#C85A32`.
  - *Under Verification:* Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#92400E`.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E5E0D5`, text `#1B2A22`. Active background `#1B4332`, border `#1B4332`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E0D5`, corner radius `8px`.
- Focus state activates an outline of `2px solid #1B4332` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.1)` glow ring.
- Placeholder text in `#8C827A` to ensure legibility on off-white surfaces.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px`, border `1.5px solid #C4BEB4`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio).
- Active state fills with `#1B4332`, rendering a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Lost & Found Components
- **Item Verification Stepper:** Progress timeline tracking `Reported → Match Found → ID Verification → Handed Over`.
- **Secure Match Claim Banner:** Surface `#F8F6F0` framed by a `#C85A32` left accent border (`4px`), prompting students to describe identifying marks or upload proof of ownership.
